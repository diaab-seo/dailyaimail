## Key Perplexity Stats

- **Perplexity had 30 million monthly active users** as of April 2025.
- **Perplexity processed around 600 million search queries per month** in April 2025.
- **The Perplexity website received 239.97 million visits worldwide** in November 2025.
- **Perplexity's mobile apps reached 80.5 million lifetime downloads**, including 5.1 million in November 2025 alone.
- **Perplexity's annual recurring revenue reached nearly $200 million** by September 2025.
- **Perplexity raised $1.22 billion across seven publicly disclosed funding rounds**, reaching a reported $20 billion valuation.

Perplexity is still much smaller than Google Search in absolute scale, but the company no longer looks like an experimental AI answer engine. The source set shows a business that has built real user traction, large query volume, substantial app distribution, and revenue momentum in a relatively short period.

The more useful way to read these numbers is as a stack rather than a single headline metric. Monthly active users show reach, query volume shows repeat behavior, traffic and app downloads show distribution, and revenue plus funding show how much investor and commercial confidence has formed around that usage.

## How Many People Use Perplexity?

The clearest top-line user number in your raw file is **30 million monthly active users** as of April 2025. That is a sharp increase from **10 million monthly active users in January 2024** and just **2 million in March 2023**. The implication is straightforward: Perplexity roughly tripled its monthly active user base between early 2024 and April 2025, and expanded fifteen-fold from the March 2023 starting point listed in the source set. [The Verge] [Perplexity]

That pace matters because it shows Perplexity building a meaningful user base despite competing in one of the hardest categories in technology: search. Unlike many AI products that rely mainly on novelty or one-off trial, search-centric products need recurring usage to become habit-forming. The query-volume data in the same source set suggests that this user base is not merely signing up. It is actually returning and asking questions at scale. [The Verge] [TechCrunch]

## Perplexity Search Volume

Perplexity's usage intensity is easiest to see in search-query growth. CEO Aravind Srinivas said the product was handling around **600 million search queries per month** as of April 2025, up from **400 million in October 2024** and **250 million in July 2024**. Those are large numbers on their own, but the more important signal is the slope: query volume more than doubled in less than a year. [The Verge] [TechCrunch]

This metric is especially useful because search products live or die on repeat behavior. A growing MAU number can still hide shallow engagement, but sustained query expansion suggests users are forming real habits around the product. In Perplexity's case, the query curve looks like a sign of deepening usage, not just top-of-funnel awareness.

## Perplexity Website Traffic

The Semrush traffic snapshot in your raw data shows that Perplexity's website reached **239.97 million visits worldwide in November 2025**, down from **276.5 million in October 2025** but still above the **194.37 million** reported in September 2025. That puts the product at a traffic level where month-to-month fluctuations matter less than overall sustained scale. [Semrush]

The November decline is worth noting because it reminds us that Perplexity's trajectory is not perfectly linear. Even fast-growing AI products can see pullbacks after spikes. But the bigger frame remains positive: by late 2025, Perplexity was generating web traffic in the hundreds of millions of visits per month, which is far beyond hobby-project territory.

## Perplexity App Downloads

Perplexity's app-distribution curve is one of the clearest growth signals in the dataset. Lifetime downloads across iOS and Android reached **80.5 million**, with **5.1 million installs in November 2025 alone**. The table shows steady low-single-digit millions through early 2025 before the curve steepened sharply in the second half of the year. [Appfigures]

The biggest monthly spike came in **October 2025 with 15.45 million app installs**, following **11.98 million in September** and **8.84 million in August**. That kind of acceleration suggests Perplexity's mobile distribution broadened materially in late 2025. The November pullback to 5.1 million is notable, but it still represents a far higher install base than the product was posting at the start of 2024.

## Perplexity Revenue and Business Scale

The strongest business datapoint in the source set is that Perplexity's annual recurring revenue reportedly reached **nearly $200 million** as of September 2025, up from **$100 million in March 2025**. That implies the company roughly doubled ARR in about six months. For a still-young AI search startup, that is a meaningful sign that the product's user growth is translating into monetizable demand. [TechCrunch] [Perplexity]

Revenue growth matters more here than valuation alone because it gives a better read on operating traction. A startup can attract investor enthusiasm on narrative, but sustained ARR expansion usually indicates that customers are finding enough value in the product to pay repeatedly. In Perplexity's case, the revenue line makes the company look less like a speculative search challenger and more like a fast-scaling software business with a search-native interface.

## Perplexity Funding and Valuation

Perplexity raised **$1.22 billion** across **seven publicly disclosed funding rounds** in the source set you provided. The listed rounds show a progression from **$28.8 million Series A in March 2023** to **$500 million Series D in December 2024**, followed by **$100 million** and **$200 million** Series E rounds in July and September 2025. [Tracxn]

That capital base helps explain how Perplexity has been able to move so aggressively in product, search infrastructure, and distribution. The related TechCrunch report in your source set also places the company at a **$20 billion valuation**, underscoring how much investor appetite has formed around AI-native search even before Perplexity approaches the scale of incumbent search platforms.
