## Key Claude Stats

- **Claude had 18.9 million monthly active users worldwide** in the latest figure provided in the source set.
- **Claude's app had 2.9 million monthly active users** in January 2025.
- **The United States and India accounted for 33.13% of Claude's website user base** in January 2025.
- **Claude reached 16 million unique website visitors** in January 2025 after peaking at 18.8 million in November 2024.
- **More than half of Claude's website audience was ages 18 to 24**, with that segment representing 51.88% of users.
- **Claude app downloads reached 769.6 thousand in January 2025**, following 800.6 thousand in November 2024.
- **Anthropic reached $850 million in annualized revenue**, with reports that it was targeting $2.2 billion in revenue for 2025.

Claude remains much smaller than ChatGPT on pure scale, but the data still shows a product that has built a meaningful global audience across both web and app surfaces. The stronger story here is not only usage growth. It is the emergence of a younger, concentrated, and internationally distributed user base around Anthropic's flagship chatbot.

The numbers also show why Claude and Anthropic need to be read together. Claude's website traffic, app usage, and demographics describe the product's reach, while Anthropic's revenue figures give the clearest read on how effectively that demand is being turned into business traction.

## How Many People Use Claude?

The lead number in your source file is **18.9 million monthly active users worldwide**. That sits above the website-only figure because the dataset also includes Claude's app audience. The strongest directly comparable traffic series in the source set is the web trend from Semrush, which shows Claude reaching **16 million unique website visitors in January 2025** after peaking at **18.8 million in November 2024**. Read together, those numbers suggest Claude's total monthly footprint remains larger than its website audience alone, with mobile usage contributing a meaningful additional layer. [Semrush] [Aicpb]

The web growth trend is also useful because it shows how quickly Claude scaled after launch. Website users rose from **4 million in December 2023** to **10.1 million in March 2024**, then continued climbing into the mid-to-high teens by late 2024. January 2025 came in below the November 2024 peak, but not by enough to change the larger picture: Claude had clearly established a multi-million global audience within less than two years of launch. ## Claude Website Traffic Trend

Claude's web audience did not grow in a straight line. There were month-to-month fluctuations, especially between spring and late summer 2024, before a stronger run-up in the fourth quarter. That pattern matters because it suggests Claude's user base is responsive to product cycles, model launches, and broader shifts in attention rather than simply compounding at a constant rate.

The late-2024 acceleration is still the clearest signal in the table. Claude moved from **13.7 million website users in September 2024** to **16.7 million in October** and **18.8 million in November** before easing to **18.3 million in December** and **16 million in January 2025**. Even with that pullback, the January result remained four times larger than the December 2023 starting point. [Semrush]

## Claude App Users and Downloads

Claude's app data shows a smaller but still meaningful mobile audience. As of January 2025, the app had **2.9 million monthly active users worldwide**, which the raw data notes ranked Claude as the **86th most used AI app worldwide**. That makes the app material, but still clearly secondary to the web footprint. [Aicpb]

Download momentum was stronger than the MAU rank might suggest. Claude app downloads moved from **679.8 thousand in August 2024** to **800.6 thousand in November 2024**, dipped to **643.3 thousand in December**, and then recovered to **769.6 thousand in January 2025**. The key point here is that Claude was still attracting new mobile users at a pace measured in hundreds of thousands per month. [Appfigures]

## Claude User Demographics

Claude's web audience skews heavily male in the source set, with **77.1% male** and **22.9% female** users. The age profile is even more concentrated. Users ages **18 to 24** account for **51.88%** of the audience, followed by the **25 to 34** group at **25%**. That means roughly three-quarters of Claude's measured website audience is under 35. [Semrush]

This matters because the product's user profile looks unusually young for a tool that is also competing for enterprise credibility. Younger users can be a growth advantage for awareness and habit formation, but they can also mean the traffic base is more experimental and less tied to long-term monetization than a more deeply enterprise-weighted audience would be.
