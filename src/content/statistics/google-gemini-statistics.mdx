## Key Gemini Stats

- **The Gemini app reached more than 750 million monthly active users** by Q4 2025.
- **AI Overviews served more than 2 billion monthly users**, giving Gemini distribution far beyond the standalone app.
- **Google sold more than 8 million paid Gemini Enterprise seats** across **2,800+ companies** by Q4 2025.
- **13 million developers had built with Google's generative AI models** by Q3 2025.
- **Gemini's estimated web traffic reached roughly 1.1 billion monthly visits**, with Similarweb-based reporting showing **157% growth** from April to September 2025.
- **Gemini captured 21.5% of global GenAI chatbot traffic** in one January 2026 Similarweb-based market share snapshot.
- **Appfigures estimated 27 million Gemini downloads in February 2026**, after **35.7 million** in December 2025.

Gemini has moved well beyond the stage where it can be treated as only a chatbot app. The source set shows a product family with several layers of reach: a large standalone app, a search distribution engine through AI Overviews, a growing enterprise seat base, and a sizable developer ecosystem inside Google Cloud and Vertex AI.

That distinction matters because Gemini's competitive position depends on more than app users alone. Google's advantage is that Gemini can scale through products people already use, which means the app, Search, Workspace, Android, and enterprise tooling all reinforce one another.

## How Many People Use Gemini?

The clearest top-line user figure in the source file is Google's report that the **Gemini app had more than 750 million monthly active users** by Q4 2025. Earlier checkpoints in the same growth path put Gemini at **400 million monthly active users in May 2025**, **450 million+ in July 2025**, and **650 million+ in October 2025**. That means Gemini added roughly **350 million monthly active users** in well under a year. [Google]

The app figure is only part of the total reach story. Google also said **AI Overviews now serve more than 2 billion monthly users** and that **AI Mode had crossed 100 million monthly active users in the US and India**, with another source point citing **75 million daily active users**. These are not interchangeable metrics, but together they show that Gemini's practical surface area now extends far beyond the standalone app. [Google]

## Gemini Enterprise and Developer Adoption

Gemini's enterprise numbers are one of the strongest signals in the file because they connect product usage to commercial traction. Google said it had sold **more than 8 million paid Gemini Enterprise seats** across **more than 2,800 companies** by Q4 2025. The file also notes that **more than 85,000 enterprises were building with Gemini** in Q2 2025 and that Gemini usage among Google Cloud customers had grown **35x year over year**. [Google]

Developer adoption is also large by any reasonable standard. Google said **9 million developers** had built with Gemini by Q2 2025, rising to **13 million developers** by Q3 2025. The Stack Overflow 2025 Developer Survey datapoints in your source set add context: **84% of respondents said they are using or planning to use AI tools in development**, **51% of professional developers said they use AI tools daily**, and **28% of professional developers said they had used Google Gemini**. [Google] [Stack Overflow]

These figures matter because they suggest Gemini is not relying only on consumer curiosity. It is increasingly part of Google's broader software stack for search, cloud, productivity, and development workflows.

## Gemini Downloads, Demographics, and Geography

The app-download data shows continuing momentum on mobile. Appfigures estimated **35.7 million downloads in December 2025** and **27 million in February 2026**, while another source in the raw file says Gemini had reached **500 million cumulative downloads** across Google Play and the App Store since May 2023. A separate November 2025 snapshot cites **64.92 million app downloads** for that month. Even with differences in methodology, the consistent signal is strong mobile demand. [Appfigures] [AppMagic] [Statista]

Audience data in the file points to a user base centered on young adults. The largest age group is **25 to 34**, and one February 2026 Similarweb snapshot shows Gemini's audience at **60.53% male** and **39.47% female**. Geographic traffic is broad, with the **United States at 14.99%**, followed by **India at 6.46%**, **Japan at 6.38%**, **South Korea at 6.22%**, and **Brazil at 5.3%** in one market breakdown. ## Gemini Market Share and Competitive Position

The market share datapoints in your source file show real momentum, even if the category still has a clear leader. Similarweb-based reporting in January 2026 put Gemini at **21.5% of global GenAI chatbot traffic**, up from **5.7% a year earlier**, while ChatGPT remained much larger at **64.5%** in the same tracker. Another comparison table in the raw data lists **Gemini at 750 million monthly active users**, against **ChatGPT at 800 million+ weekly active users**, which are very different metrics but still show Gemini operating at top-tier scale. [Similarweb] [Google]

This is probably the most accurate way to frame Gemini competitively: it is still smaller than ChatGPT by the most visible headline metrics, but it is large enough, fast-growing enough, and distributed widely enough across Google's ecosystem to be treated as a durable top-tier rival rather than a niche secondary player.

## What the Data Tells Us

The most important signal in this dataset is that Gemini is scaling on multiple layers at once. The standalone app is large, Search gives the product enormous distribution, enterprise seats are converting attention into paid adoption, and developer usage suggests Gemini is becoming part of day-to-day software workflows.

For that reason, Gemini's story in 2026 is not just about whether it can match ChatGPT on a single top-line user metric. It is about whether Google can turn ecosystem reach into sustained engagement, enterprise revenue, and long-term platform leverage. The source set suggests that process is already well underway.
